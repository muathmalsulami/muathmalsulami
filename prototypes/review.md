## Recommendation

B is the strongest candidate for approval. A is the most expressive at a glance, and C makes evidence easiest to scan. B ties the visual identity to the actual work across more of the profile: application UI, model integration, observability, evidence and shipping. An experienced engineer can inspect why the stages exist, while a recruiter can skim the early map and follow a project link. C is the stronger choice when recruitment speed outweighs standalone brand recognition. These are review judgments, with no measured recruiter conversion or recognition claim.

## Refinements and limitations

- **A** moved the artwork adjacent to positioning, shortened the opening, and rerouted the final stroke so it does not obscure the M. The work-first reading path remains the longest route to skills.
- **B** differentiated the three module faces, compressed navigation and split motion into seating, connection and one traversal. The small station details are intentionally redundant with native content.
- **C** put honors and education access in the opening, shortened navigation and moved status and case links ahead of the project art. The compact mobile table keeps its semantic relationships but requires multiline cells.

The target of 95–97 is still open. Independent five-second and mark-recognition tests, physical-device and screen-reader testing, and stronger inspectable evidence could change the judgment. Exact degree wording is carried forward from the prior draft. The [Umm Al-Qura catalog](https://uqu.edu.sa/en/App/Degrees?dept=1&faculty=14) lists programs under a shared department and does not establish the wording on this personal credential. LinkedIn blocked anonymous verification of the existing URL. The portfolio, case-study dialogs, portfolio source and Pathy certificate were rechecked during this pass and resolved successfully.

## Browser evidence

Anonymous Microsoft Edge loaded the actual branch pages at 320, 390 and 1440 CSS pixels in both themes. The six SVGs per README loaded and maintained their aspect ratios, with no page or README overflow. All local heading links resolved. Keyboard navigation to Selected work passed. Three 390 px image-blocked tests retained the name, education and project text. The scoped axe WCAG A/AA scan found zero violations under the tested rules. The still source was confirmed by URL and pixel comparisons, and the animated source resolved to a stable final frame. The six animated-mode assets total roughly 11–13 kB before transport compression. Solid-field SVG text pairs measured at least 4.78:1 across the tested palettes. A complete eight-width matrix is reserved for the approved design.
